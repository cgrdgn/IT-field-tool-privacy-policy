Summary: IT Field Tool does not have user accounts and does not operate any server. Every scan, calculation and lookup result stays on your device. We do not collect, store or transmit your network data. The only data leaving your device are DNS queries you initiate and standard advertising requests handled by Google AdMob.

1. Who we are

IT Field Tool: IP & Subnet ("the app") is developed and published by [GELİŞTİRİCİ ADI — Play Console'daki geliştirici adınla aynı olmalı] ("we", "us"). You can reach us at [E-POSTA ADRESİN].

2. Data we collect ourselves

None. We operate no backend, no database and no analytics service. We have no way to identify you, and we never receive your scan results, IP addresses, hostnames or any other network information.

3. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, using Android's standard preferences storage. This data never leaves your device and is permanently deleted when you uninstall the app.

  • Your recent scan history (last 5 entries in the free version, unlimited with Pro)
  • App settings such as language and theme
  • Counters used to decide when to show the optional Pro offer
  • Your Pro purchase status

4. Data processed by the tools

ToolWhat happens
Subnet CalculatorPure local arithmetic. No network connection is made.
DNS LookupYour query is sent over an encrypted HTTPS connection to a public DNS resolver (Cloudflare DNS or Google Public DNS) to retrieve the record. The domain or IP you enter is visible to that resolver.
Port ScannerTCP connection attempts to the host you specify. Results stay on your device.
PingICMP or TCP probes to the host you specify. Results stay on your device.
LAN DiscoveryScans your own local network for reachable devices. Results stay on your device. The manufacturer lookup uses an offline database bundled inside the app; no request is made for this.

Apart from DNS Lookup, none of the tools transmit anything to us or to any third party.

5. Permissions and why they are needed

  • INTERNET, ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE — required for every diagnostic function and for serving ads.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE — reads your current Wi-Fi connection details so the app can determine your local subnet.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION — not used for location tracking. Since Android 10, the operating system requires this permission before an app can read the Wi-Fi network name (SSID) and related details. Your geographic position is never requested, recorded or transmitted by us.
  • NEARBY_WIFI_DEVICES — the Android 13+ replacement for the above, used for the same purpose.
  • AD_ID — allows Google AdMob to access the advertising identifier.

6. Advertising

The free version of the app displays advertisements through Google AdMob. AdMob is operated by Google LLC and acts as an independent data controller for the data it collects. It may collect and process:

  • Your device's advertising identifier (AAID)

  • Ad interaction data such as impressions and clicks

Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy and the Google Ads Data Processing Terms.

Your consent choices

You can change your choice at any time from the app's Settings screen.

You can also reset or delete your advertising identifier at any time in your device settings under Settings → Google → Ads.

Removing ads

Purchasing the one-time Pro unlock removes all advertising from the app. Once Pro is active, no ad is requested or displayed.

7. Purchases

8. Children

9. Data retention and your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to retain, export or erase on your behalf. All app data resides on your device and is removed completely when you uninstall the app. You may also clear it at any time through your device's application settings.

For data held by Google as a result of advertising or purchases, please exercise your rights directly with Google using the links above.
